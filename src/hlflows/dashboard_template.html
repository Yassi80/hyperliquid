▲/▼ show direction. Delta and CVD exist only while the recorder was live; "—" means no data for that bar; ≈ marks a delta missing some minutes of the bar (e.g. a reconnect). Watch Δnet = change in the watchlist's net position over the bar (same wallets at both ends, USD at close). Liquidations are partial (watched wallets + backstops).

Each panel has its own scale. Blue = buy / long / up, red = sell / short / down. CVD is anchored at the left edge of the window. A bar missing a few minutes of taker data (a reconnect) still counts, and the readout (top right, while you hover) shows in brackets how much of the bar was covered; breaks in a line are bars with no taker data at all, after which the sum resumes. Watchlist positions are in coin units (they also move as wallets join or leave the watchlist); the net-flow panel counts only wallets present at both ends of each bar. Footprint: behind each candle, every price level is shaded by its taker delta (blue = more aggressive buying, red = more selling, stronger = larger), and the dash marks the bar's busiest level (point of control). Zoom in to about a dozen candles (mouse wheel over the price panel, or drag across the chart; double-click to zoom back out) to see sells×buys per level in coin units (levels merge as needed to fit the numbers); bold marks a diagonal imbalance (buys at a level ≥ 3× sells one level below, or the reverse). Hovering the price panel shows the level under the cursor in the readout.
