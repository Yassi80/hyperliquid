▲/▼ show direction. Delta and CVD exist only while the recorder was live; "—" means no data for that bar; ≈ marks a delta missing some minutes of the bar (e.g. a reconnect). Watch Δnet = change in the watchlist's net position over the bar (same wallets at both ends, USD at close). Liquidations are partial (watched wallets + backstops).

Each panel has its own scale. Blue = buy / long / up, red = sell / short / down. CVD is anchored at the left edge of the window. A bar missing a few minutes of taker data (a reconnect) still counts, and its hover says how much of the bar was covered; breaks in a line are bars with no taker data at all, after which the sum resumes. Watchlist notional also moves as wallets join or leave the watchlist; the net-flow panel counts only wallets present at both ends of each bar.
